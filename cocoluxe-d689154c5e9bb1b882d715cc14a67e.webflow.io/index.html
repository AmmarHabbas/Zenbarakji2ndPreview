Small Batches with Great Taste

We're a team of chocolatiers hand-crafting exquisite confections in Damscus, Syria. At Zenbarakji, our mission is to create the most decadent, luxurious, and unforgettable homemade bonbon chocolates in Damascus. We are committed to using only the finest ingredients, including high-quality chocolate and locally sourced, organic, and sustainable ingredients whenever possible. We believe that our passion for chocolate and dedication to craftsmanship are what sets us apart, and we strive to deliver an exceptional experience with every bite. Our goal is to spread joy, happiness, and indulgence with every truffle bonbon chocolate we make, and to create a community of chocolate lovers who share our appreciation for the art of fine chocolate-making.
